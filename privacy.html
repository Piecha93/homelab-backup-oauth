Homelab backup privacy policy

Effective: 20 October 2026.

Homelab backup is operated by Piecha93 for personal use.

Google Drive access

The application requests permission to read and manage Google Drive files. The configured backup jobs list, create, read, update and delete backup and recovery files. Account identity and storage information may be read to verify the destination and available space.

Storage and use

Backup contents are encrypted on the owner's device before upload to the owner's Google Drive. Authorization credentials are stored on the owner's devices and in an encrypted recovery package. They are not published on this website.

Google API data is used only to operate backups, retention and recovery. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. No separate operator receives the backup contents.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention and can delete backup files through Google Drive. Access can be revoked at Google Account connections. Revoking access stops automated backups until authorization is restored.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics, advertising or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process connection information under GitHub's privacy statement. Google Drive operates under Google's privacy policy.

Contact the operator through the GitHub profile.
